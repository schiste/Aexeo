# Choosing a backup schedule

In today's fast-paced world, teams need a backup schedule that matches how they work. A schedule starts with a practical question: how much recent work could the team afford to recreate?

## Recovery goals

List the systems and files people need to restore. Give each one an owner, then agree on how quickly it needs to be available after an incident.

## Test the plan

Restore a sample into a separate environment. Record the result, assign follow-up work and repeat the test after meaningful changes to the system.
