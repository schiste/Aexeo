# Synthetic workflow fixtures

The before/after fixtures were deliberately constructed to exercise known Aexeo checks.
The open article was drafted without a seeded defect; its bounded revisions use
an intentionally unreasonable preset to exercise retry exhaustion.
They are not independently labelled examples and must not be used as a human
holdout or to estimate detection accuracy. See
[`docs/plans/EDITORIAL-AGENT-TRIAL.md`](../../docs/plans/EDITORIAL-AGENT-TRIAL.md)
for the procedure, observations and limitations.
